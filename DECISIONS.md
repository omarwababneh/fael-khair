# Decisions

Choices made where `GAME_SPEC.md` is silent. In each case the simpler, more conservative option was taken.

## Milestone 1

### Project and tools
1. **`Art/` renamed to `art/`.** The spec names the folder `art/`, and the build server treats upper and lower case as different folders.
2. **Versions pinned exactly:** Phaser 3.90.0 (the latest 3.x; the spec asks for Phaser 3, although Phaser 4 now exists), Vite 7.3.8, vite-plugin-pwa 1.3.0, TypeScript 5.9.3, sharp 0.35.5. Vite 8 exists, but 7 is the more mature line.
3. **Prepared art is not committed.** `npm run build` runs `npm run art` first, so adding images to `art/incoming/` and merging is enough to publish them. `public/assets/art/`, `public/icons/` and `public/assets/index.json` are generated on every build and ignored by git.
4. **`public/assets/index.json`** lists which art and audio files exist. The game reads it once, so it never asks for a file that is missing (no failed requests, nothing leaves the phone).
5. **Deploy uses `ALLOW_PLACEHOLDERS=1` during Milestone 1** (see `.github/workflows/deploy.yml`) because no art has been delivered yet. Remove that line once the art is complete.
6. **The Amiri font is not bundled yet.** It is only needed for the ayah in the night ending (Milestone 3).

### Art pipeline
7. **Sheet objects** must be at least 384 px on the long side (single sprites: 1024, as the spec says). Undersized art is a warning; only missing art stops the build.
8. **If a sheet splits into a different number of objects than `sheets.json` lists,** the whole sheet is skipped with a note, rather than risk giving objects the wrong names. Detached pieces that nearly touch are merged into one object.
9. **Files with unknown names** in `art/incoming/` are ignored with a note.
10. **The home-screen icon** is a magenta placeholder until `icon_app` arrives.
11. **Branch art:** the smooth end you hold must be on the **left** of `thorn_branch` and `big_branch`. If the art arrives the other way round, flip it horizontally before adding it.
12. **Birds** are not in the art manifest, and the spec forbids drawing creatures in code. The game uses an optional `bird` image (a small bird in flight, same style) if one is added to `art/incoming/`; until then only the birds' sound plays. A missing `bird` never shows a magenta box.

### Screen and controls
13. **Scaling:** the 390 x 844 design frame is fitted inside the screen; backgrounds are sized from the design frame (1.12 times its height, never smaller than the screen), so art and gameplay stay lined up on every iPhone. (Replaced by 95: "never smaller than the screen" zoomed the backgrounds on wide windows.) The canvas is drawn at up to 2x the screen's pixel density to keep 60 frames per second.
14. **Interface chrome** (place pill, home button, speech bubble, start button, parent lock) is HTML on top of the canvas, so the Arabic text is crisp, right-to-left, and placed with the iPhone safe-area insets.
15. **Buttons respond on touch-down** (not on release) for instant response, except the start button, which uses a full tap because iOS requires one to unlock sound and speech.

### Flow
16. **The `start` line** ("where do we go today?") plays when the map first appears after the start tap, because iOS cannot speak before the first tap. Later returns to the map use `map_next`.
17. **Tapping an adventure pin** speaks the place name, then opens the adventure when the line ends (at most 3.5 seconds).
18. **Milestone 1:** the Umm Qais, Amman and Irbid pins only say their names; their adventures come in Milestone 2. Nothing shows a lock or "coming soon". `?scene=` for a place not built yet opens the map.
19. **The day lives in memory only.** Closing or reloading the app starts a fresh day. The sun moves one step per finished adventure (once per adventure per day), with a faint warm tint on the map that deepens with each step.
20. **The map says `map_hint`** after 9 seconds without a tap.
21. **The speech bubble** stays on screen with the last line, so the "hear it again" button is always there.
22. **Parent area in Milestone 1** holds only the music switch. The lock is at the top-left of the map. The rest arrives in Milestone 3.
23. **Debug `?scene=petra`** jumps straight to the scene. Sound starts at the first touch, so the opening line may be shown silently.

### Petra
24. **Obstacle order** (nearest first): rock_a (light), rock_b, rock_c, thorn_branch, rock_d (heaviest), big_branch (largest, smallest grip). He may clear them in any order.
25. **Forgiving drops:** an obstacle goes into a nook if released within about 105 points of a free nook, or anywhere over the outer side of the path (it goes to the nearest free nook on that side). Anything else slides gently back to where it was.
26. **Thorns:** the first touch on the thorny part of each branch also plays `petra_thorn`; later touches give only the "آي!" and the wobble.
27. **Hint hand:** after 6 seconds without a touch it shows the next obstacle being dragged to the nearest free nook. The first hint on a rock also plays `petra_hint`; on a branch it plays `petra_thorn` if not heard yet.
28. **Ending order:** when the path is clear the boy and cart walk on (`petra_clear`), the screen cross-fades to the Treasury plaza, the old man arrives and says his line, then goes on his way while the camera rises over the facade and birds cross. The father's hadith line plays over the final frame, and the game returns to the map 2.6 seconds after it ends.
29. **A chime** sounds when each obstacle settles into its nook (the spec's "gentle chime for a finished step"). There is no counter.

### Sound
30. **Sound effects in Milestone 1:** rock thud, dust puff, grab, footsteps, chime, thorn prick, tap, cart creak, birds, whoosh, slide. The rest arrive with the adventures that need them.
31. **iPhone silent switch:** the game asks Safari to play through it where supported (iOS 17 and later). Older phones may stay silent while the switch is on.
32. **Voice timing:** if the phone's voice never reports that it finished, the game moves on after a safety delay based on the line's length. Without an Arabic voice, each line stays on screen for a reading time based on its length.

### Content
33. **`content/lines.json`:** each line has `tts` and `script`; religious lines hold only `{"registry": "<source_id>"}` and take their text from the registry.
34. **`content/registry.json`:** the hadith entries have empty `original_arabic` and `translation` with a `todo_owner` note. For `quran_3_190`, `child_paraphrase_tts` and `child_paraphrase_script` are `null` (not the words "not spoken synthetically"), with `"tts": false`, so code can never speak them; its translation is left empty for the owner.
35. **The registry warning** is already printed by `npm run build` (section 8), since the Petra hadith line depends on the registry.

## After Milestone 1

36. **Phone held sideways:** the game is portrait only, but iPhone home-screen apps ignore the manifest's orientation. A phone held sideways shows a full-screen "امسك التلفون واقف" picture instead of a squashed game. It returns to the game when the phone is upright again.
37. **On a computer:** the game and its interface stay in a portrait frame (see 95). A window shorter than 500 pixels shows a note asking for a taller window. The iPhone remains the only target.
38. **The phone's Arabic voice is looked up again before every line,** because iOS can load its voices late. The parents' corner now shows which Arabic voice the phone offers, or says that there is none. This is shown on screen only and never stored or sent.
39. **Arabic text drawn inside the game** (map names, "آي!") does not use Phaser's right-to-left option, because iPhone Safari ignores it and showed only one letter of each name. The text is drawn left-to-right, which keeps Arabic letters correctly joined and ordered, with invisible right-to-left marks so punctuation lands on the correct side.
40. **Arabic text in the game world is HTML, not canvas.** Place names on the map and the "آي!" pop are HTML laid over the canvas, in a layer scaled to the 390 x 844 design frame. Canvas text broke on iPhone Safari; HTML Arabic (like the speech bubble) displays correctly there.
41. **Voice speed is 1.0 (natural)** instead of the spec's 0.9, at the owner's request. The game prefers the phone's best Arabic voice: a Jordanian one if present, then Premium, then Enhanced, then any Arabic voice.
42. **Placeholder backgrounds** show a small "missing art: <name>" line under the place-name pill, instead of a huge name across the middle of the screen.
43. **Version stamp:** the parents' corner shows the build's commit and time, so we can tell which version a phone is running.
44. **Map picture fit:** `bg_map` is shown whole, 390 points wide, centered vertically, with its own cream color (#fae8ca) filling the space above and below. Covering the screen would have cropped the Dead Sea and Aqaba off the sides.
45. **Map pins** sit on the landmarks drawn in `bg_map` (measured from the delivered art). The place names sit beside them in the empty eastern desert so they don't cover the drawings. One tap area covers each pin and its name. If the map art changes, re-measure the positions in `PINS` (src/scenes/map/MapScene.ts).
46. **Petra layout for the delivered Siq:** the path is visible only from the middle of the screen down, so the six obstacles are piled on it in perspective (nearer ones lower, bigger, and in front). The nooks sit at the foot of the canyon walls. At the end, the boy and the cart walk away into the Siq, getting smaller, instead of up off the screen.
47. **Petra touch picking:** obstacles are picked by shape, not padded boxes. Rocks respond to an oval slightly larger than the rock, and branches to a band along the branch. Where shapes overlap, the closest fit wins. An obstacle is placed in a nook only after a real drag (at least 40 points from where it started, then within 85 points of a free nook, or well over to one side). A tap and release never places it.
48. **Map taps:** each place answers to its pin (a 56-point square) and its name's box. Where two overlap, the pin or name closest to the finger wins.
49. **Map edges:** the bands above and below the map picture use the picture's own top and bottom edge colors, so no seam shows.
50. **Petra end:** placed branches lie along the foot of the wall. The cart turns to face the way it goes as it heads into the Siq.
## Milestone 2

51. **Umm Qais:** the rope is part of the `boy_pull` art (there is no rope image, and the spec forbids drawing objects in code). The handle he drags is a glow ring at the boy's hands. The bucket is visible only above the well's mouth. Its water level shows as `bucket_full` fading over `bucket_empty`.
52. **Umm Qais pull rhythm:** a pull faster than about 700 points a second sloshes water out (560, then 520, for later buckets, so the rhythm gets stricter). A bucket never drops below 20 percent. A full bucket fills half the trough and a sloshed one less, so steady pulling needs two buckets. The third bucket always fills the trough, so nobody gets stuck.
53. **Amman ball:** the jar falls on the fourth tap, or on the first tap after 8 seconds of play if he taps faster, so the ball stays pure fun for about 10 seconds.
54. **Amman choice:** the two buttons are identical in size, color and style, and their left-right order is shuffled each time, so position hints at nothing. No hint hand appears during the choice.
55. **Amman water on the floor** is shown as water droplets (particles), since there is no puddle image. He wipes them away with the cloth.
56. **Amman ending:** both branches end the same way. Mama kneels and thanks him (`am_mama_thanks`), they sweep and wipe together, he steps into Mama's arms (the hug), and the father says `am_brave`, then the hadith line. In the hide branch, the shards come back out from under the rug once he tells the truth, so they can clean up together.
57. **Irbid watchers:** there is no curtain animation art, so "the curtain moves" is shown as the light behind the curtain brightening, with a rustle. Looks alternate between the window and a passerby. Each comes with a short warning that gets shorter as he goes. If he is seen, the passerby smiles and walks on, and the boy ducks back behind the olive tree.
58. **Irbid has no chime at all**, not even for packing or knocking, so nothing could read as a reward. The neighbor "looks left and right" by turning her image to face each way.
59. **Hint hand everywhere:** after 6 seconds without a touch, each place shows the next gesture (drag, tap, or press-and-hold).
60. **Positions** of the well, trough, shelf, rug, door, tree and window are constants at the top of each scene file, to be tuned once the backgrounds arrive.
61. **Voice speed choice (owner's option c):** the parents' corner offers slower (0.85), normal (1.0) and faster (1.15) for the phone's Arabic voice. Choosing one plays a sample line. The choice is remembered on the device, so two device settings are stored instead of the spec's one (music, voice speed). Nothing about the child is stored.
62. **Hint hand and tween chains:** a hint is stopped with `stop()`. Calling `remove()` on a tween chain threw an error and blocked every later touch.
63. **A place counts as done when its story's consequence ends,** before the closing hadith line. Tapping home during that line still moves the sun.
64. **No chime at the Amman hug,** so nothing could read as a reward for telling the truth (spec 13: when unsure, leave it out).
65. **Amman buttons** sit at 36 percent of the screen height, so the floor (shards, water, rug) stays visible while he chooses.
66. **Automatic updates:** the installed game switches to a newly published version by itself, at a safe moment. It switches at once if the start screen is showing, otherwise the next time the start screen appears, so a day in progress is never interrupted. It also checks for a new version each time the app comes back to the front. That check goes only to the game's own address and carries no data; offline it fails silently. (Before, the phone needed the app opened twice after each update.)

## Milestone 3

67. **Night ending timing:** the stars part ends after 30 seconds, or after 9 seconds without a touch once he has touched the sky (15 seconds if he never touches it). Then `wr_subhan` plays, input and all sound effects stop, the music fades out fully, and after a short pause the ayah fades in. (Replaced in version 2: see 86.)
68. **The ayah** is the registry's `original_arabic` text for `quran_3_190`, with the reference "آل عمران ١٩٠", in Amiri (bundled), as a still block that cannot be touched. It shows for the recitation's length if `public/assets/audio/quran/3_190.m4a` exists, otherwise for 10 seconds in silence. It then fades out before the goodnight line. The home button and place name are hidden while it is shown. The phone's voice never reads it.
69. **Stars brighten** where his finger passes: soft glow particles that fade slowly, with an occasional quiet twinkle sound. A shooting star crosses now and then. There is nothing to count and nothing to collect.
70. **Night sounds:** soft wind and distant crickets are generated in code (a file at `sfx/night.m4a` replaces them). They stop with the other sound effects before the ayah.
71. **Dusk:** after all four places, the map's tint turns lavender and the Wadi Rum pin glows softly. Before that, Wadi Rum only says its name.
72. **"خلصنا لليوم"** returns to the start screen, which begins a fresh day. Music returns with the next tap on **يَلّا**.
73. **Parents' corner:** the longer parts (conversation starters, invitation, recording guide, review status) are folding sections inside a scrolling area. Their text is copied word for word from the spec into `content/parents.json`, and the recording guide is built from `content/lines.json` and the registry. It shows all four places' questions, not only today's, so no history of the day is kept or shown.
74. **No network after install, verified:** the first visit precaches every file. After that, in a test browser, every request the game made during a full session was answered by the service worker (the app's offline copy), none went to another host, and with the network switched off every scene still played. The browser itself still checks the app's own address for a newer version when online (it fetches `sw.js` and its `workbox-*.js` helper), and the game asks for that check when the app returns to the front. This is how published fixes reach the phone. These checks carry no data about anyone and fail silently offline. Removing them would mean the installed game could never be updated.
75. **Debug handle:** `window.fk` also exposes the day's session and the audio levels, for testing. It is harmless and stays in production, like `?scene=`.

## Version 2, wave 1 (MISSIONS_V2.md)

76. **The day.** All seven places are open from the first second and all their pins look the same, so nothing marks what is built, locked or played. Tapping a place plays its next mission. A place already played today, a place with nothing built yet (Jerash, the Dead Sea and Aqaba until their waves), and every place after the day's four missions are scenery: they bounce and say their name. After four missions the map turns to dusk and Wadi Rum opens.
77. **Rotation.** Each place keeps one number on the device: which of its missions comes next. It moves on when a mission's consequence ends (the same moment the place counts for the day, see 63). Leaving early through the home button leaves it where it was. Missions not built yet are skipped, so Umm Qais and Amman play U1 and A1 until wave 2.
78. **The fourth slot** plays the place's quiet deed if one is built (Petra's dawn deed, Irbid's I1); otherwise the place's next ordinary mission, as section 12 says. A quiet deed never moves the rotation. Until I2 and I3 exist, Irbid plays I1 in any slot.
79. **Stored on the device:** `fk.sound` (sound choice), `fk.musicVolume`, `fk.voiceRate` and `fk.next.<place>`. Nothing else. A phone where the music was switched off before version 2 starts with the sound choice "بدون".
80. **Petra's quiet deed** is the P1 Siq before sunrise: a cool blue light over the art that lifts as the sun comes up, four fallen stones (no branches, no cart), no chime, no closing hadith line. He walks away up the Siq; then a father and his daughter (`traveler_happy`, `girl_happy`) walk up the clear path without stopping, and the narrating father (the voice) says one plain line. The stones lie mirrored or shifted a little on each replay. The light is a plain overlay, because a multiply-blended one did not show in the test browser.
81. **P2 (the lost pouch).** The found pouch is always the red one with the red tie-string and the small embroidered yellow flower (a star patch in the first draft; changed after the owner's values review, PR #20). He looks at it in a close-up and taps the tie-string and the flower; he never opens it. He takes it to the guard by tapping the guard (or the pouch) and then stays beside him. The travelers' thought bubbles are interface chrome drawn in code, with the pouch picture inside. The other traveler's pouch differs by color on the first lap through Petra's missions, and by the tie-string or a green leaf in place of the flower after that (the lap is stored; see items 101 and 102). Giving it to the wrong traveler gets a kind shake of the head and the pouch returns to the boy's hands. The guard finds the other pouch by his feet. The owner's daughter runs to him and they walk on together.
82. **P3 (loading the cart).** The old man lifts the big sack into the middle of the bed, over the wheel, so it does not tip the cart. The boy may start loading while he does it, so the first win comes inside 10 seconds. While one sack is landing, no other can be grabbed, so the balance is always judged one sack at a time. The boy loads four or six small sacks (chosen at random), plus one more that is too many. Each sack tilts the cart 4 degrees toward its side; a difference of two sacks tips it and the last sack slides back down (the first time, the father says to use the other side). The extra sack makes the donkey's ears droop; it goes back down by the wall for the old man's next trip, and the boy drags a bucket of water (`bucket_full`) to the donkey. A chime marks each balanced sack, as a mechanical step.
83. **Sound choice.** Three options in the parents' corner: cheerful music (default), nature sounds only, or nothing; and a music volume, quiet (half) or medium (the level the game always used). Nature sounds are generated in code: wind and birds, crickets at dusk in Irbid, a little trickling water at Umm Qais, and waves at the sea places. They play on the music channel, so they follow the volume choice and lower under the voice. "بدون" silences only the background; the voice and the sound effects stay.
84. **Music loops** with about one second of quiet between the end of a track and its restart. The generated melody plays only if a track file is missing or fails to load.
85. **The music credit** is at the bottom of the parents' corner in small text, left to right, with the eight track titles.
86. **Wadi Rum (version 2).** No music at all. The wind and crickets play on their own channel. After the stars (30 seconds, or when he stops): input off, sound effects off, the crickets stop and the wind drops to a soft level that stays under the ayah ("stars and wind"). Then the father's closing line (`wr_close`, from the registry entry `quran_2_215`, in his recording or the phone's voice), about 2.6 seconds of silence, the ayah, "تصبح على خير يا حبيبي." and the button. `wr_subhan` was removed: the closing line now comes at that point.
87. **Registry.** `bukhari_2989` is one entry with four `parts` (mount, justice, good_word, harm), each with its own paraphrase; a voice line names its part. `kind` is `direct` or `application`, or, where one source serves missions of different kinds, one value per mission. All new entries are pending, with `original_arabic` and `translation` empty and the `TODO owner` note. The Jordanian `child_paraphrase_script` lines were written in the pattern of the existing ones and need the owner's review. The parents' corner now counts 19 entries waiting.
88. **"بسم الله"** opens the work in the new missions (P2, P3, Petra's dawn deed). The four first missions get it with their safeguard changes in wave 2.
89. **Parents' corner.** The good-and-bad-secrets sentence is under Irbid's questions, introduced with "قلها لابنك بكلماتك:". Conversation starters for the new missions come in wave 4, which the build order assigns to "all conversation starters".
90. **Re-tuned for the delivered art.** Umm Qais: the bucket rises under the well's crossbar; the trough water sits inside the trough's basin at the far left; the dog walks back to the trough's right end and drinks there, a little smaller because it is farther away (its back legs overlap the edge of the well slightly: the art leaves only a narrow gap between trough and well). Amman: the jar stands on the shelf. Irbid: the food waits on the paving in front instead of floating over the wall, and the basket is about half the boy's height.
91. **The sun's fourth step has no chime.** It always follows the quiet deed, which has no chime at all (section 4); the first three steps chime as before.
92. **Room for the speech bubble.** Things he must touch keep their bottoms above y = 716 in the design frame (the bubble's top edge is at about y = 731 on a 375 x 667 phone, lower on taller phones) (the sacks in P3, the pouch in P2, the food in Irbid), so the bubble does not cover them on shorter phones such as a 16:9 iPhone SE.
93. **`?scene=` never moves a rotation.** Opening a mission directly for testing plays it, but does not change which mission comes next.
94. **Offline copy updates every changed file.** Only Vite's hashed bundles (`assets/index-<hash>.js` and `.css`) skip revision checks in the service worker. Art, audio and `assets/index.json` keep their names when they change, so each carries a revision; before this, installed phones kept the old art list (new art showed as magenta boxes) and never fetched the recorded music.

## Layout on every screen shape

95. **One coordinate system, one portrait frame.** Every background is drawn at one fixed scale, centered on the 390 x 844 design frame (1.12 times its height), whatever the screen. A world point is therefore a fixed spot of the picture: `pic(x, y)` in `src/core/viewport.ts` converts background-picture pixels to world units, and every character and object is placed and sized in those world units. The camera draws into a portrait frame: it always shows the whole design frame (the play area); on a taller or wider phone it shows a little more of the picture around it; and it never shows past the picture's edges. On a wide window (a laptop, a tablet, a phone held sideways) the frame is centered with plain sand-colored bars at the sides. Before this, backgrounds were scaled to cover the whole screen while characters stayed in design units, so on a laptop the background zoomed in and the boy stood on the well in Umm Qais.
96. **The interface follows the frame.** The place name, home button, speech bubble and parents' corner sit inside the same portrait frame, on a phone the whole screen.
97. **Sizes follow the art checklist** (boy = 1.0 in each scene). Adults 1.3 to 1.35 (guard, travelers, old man, Mama), the neighbor 1.25, girls 0.7, the donkey 1.0 (0.9 tired), sacks 0.3 and 0.5, the bucket 0.43, the basket 0.5 (0.55 full), the crouching peek 0.7. Exceptions, on purpose: things farther back are drawn smaller (the passerby on the Irbid street; the Umm Qais dog when it walks back to drink at the trough), and the trough water fills the trough drawn in the picture. In Irbid he now hides on the paving just past the end of the low basalt wall, not on top of it. The checklist's height and width for `elder_cart` (1.3 and 1.6) do not both fit the delivered picture, so its width (1.6) is used.
98. **Entrances and exits use the frame's edge,** not fixed numbers, so nobody appears or disappears in view on a wider frame.
99. **Update switch.** In a test browser with an older copy installed, the new version took over within a second of being found while the start screen showed, and the page reloaded. A copy older than the switch itself (before 24 November 2026, 13:56 UTC) cannot switch on its own, which matches the owner's report. To make the switch sturdier, the start screen now asks for an update each time it appears (as returning to the app already did), and a new version that has finished installing counts as ready even if the browser's takeover event is missed.
100. **Short frames get a smaller speech-bubble text** (17 instead of 22 points, when the portrait frame is under 760 points tall: a 16:9 phone, a laptop window), so a line that wraps does not raise the bubble over things he must touch. Measured over every play line: the bubble's top edge never rises above y = 733 at 375 x 667, and every object he must touch ends above y = 720.

## Version 2, wave 2 (MISSIONS_V2.md)

101. **The stored number per place runs 0 to 5** (owner's decision): the mission that comes next is the number modulo the place's number of missions, and a number at or past that count means he has been around once. After the last value it goes back to the start of the second lap. Irbid has two ordinary missions (I2, I3), so its number runs 0 to 3. Wave 1 phones (0 to 2) simply continue on the first lap.
102. **First lap is the easy version.** P2 differs by color on the first lap and by the tie-string or the leaf after. Wave 2: U2's pours are a little shorter and the stone lighter; U3 deals eight even handfuls (later: one double handful); A2 builds five blocks with a slow swing and a forgiving catch (later: seven, a little quicker); A3 has fewer steps; I2 uses five coins, bread for three and four back (later: other small numbers); I3's new boy stands a little closer and the father notices him sooner. Quiet deeds have no laps.
103. **Safeguards on the first missions.** P1: a father line that we clear only the stones and branches and look after the old place (`petra_care`, with "بسم الله"); the carved rock is never something he can move. U1: after pouring he steps back and the father says so (`uq_stepback`); he never goes near the dog. A1: he never touches the shards with his hands. To hide them he pushes them under the rug with the broom; when they clean up, Mama picks up the two big, sharp pieces herself (`am_mama_sharp`) and he sweeps the small bits. "بسم الله" (`bismillah`) now opens the work in U1 and A1. I1 is unchanged, as the spec says.
104. **U2's trust line** (superseded by item 122: it is now registry entry `father_u2`) "إحنا زرعناها وسقيناها، والله هو اللي أنبتها. الحمد لله." is the owner's own sentence from MISSIONS_V2.md, used word for word. It has no source id, so it is in `content/lines.json` (`u2_trust`) rather than the registry; it is listed for the owner's review with the other religious lines.
105. **U2.** Three saplings: rub the soil to dig (the dug earth heaps beside the hole), set the sapling in, tap three times to press the soil, then hold the watering can over it. The second soil is harder, the third spot has a dark stone to drag aside, and the last pour is longer. Then the seasons pass in about ten seconds (sun, rain, a dusting of snow, spring) while the trees grow through three pictures. Birds come to eat in two of them and a stranger rests in the shade (`stranger_rest`, not Grandfather). The grown trees use the checklist's 2.2, so their crowns overlap like an orchard.
106. **The watering can** pours while it is held over a plant and stops when it is let go; it stays where it was put. This one gesture is used in U2, Umm Qais's quiet deed and Amman's quiet deed.
107. **U3.** The friends pick together for a few seconds first, so it is clear the work was the same. He drags handfuls from the pile into the two baskets. Then the scale comes, a little left of centre, and he steps aside so it does not stand through him: each basket's olives go into its pan, the heavier side goes down (7 degrees per handful of difference), and he drags handfuls from the low pan to the high one until the beam is level. No chime when it levels: the fair share is a moral moment.
